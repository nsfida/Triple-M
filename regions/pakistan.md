## PKR is supported for finance workflows and regional subscription presentation in Pakistan.

Triplem VIP keeps operational finance, Accounting ERP, reporting and multi-currency workflows available in one browser-based workspace. The currency used for a wallet or record remains part of that record’s financial context rather than being treated as a decorative symbol.

Use wallets, expenses, receiving, transfers, inventory, loans, installments, assets and notes for day-to-day financial organization.

### Accounting ERP

Use double-entry books, journals, receivables, payables, reconciliation, VAT analysis and financial statements when formal accounting is required.

### Permission-aware AI

Eligible users can use Triplem AI against authorized workspace data, with source references and reviewable Draft workflows rather than silent financial changes.

## International use is not limited to one market.

Current supported finance currencies include AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR and INR, with BTC available for supported Bitcoin workflows. If another currency is required, users can contact Triplem VIP and request that support be reviewed.

The availability of a currency does not mean Triplem VIP makes jurisdiction-specific tax filings or guarantees local statutory compliance.

## Keep local tax and statutory decisions anchored to official guidance.

Triplem VIP can organize records and provide VAT or tax-analysis tools where supported, but users remain responsible for applying current local law, filing rules and professional advice to their own business.
