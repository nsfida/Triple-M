Pricing FAQs

Questions before you activate a plan.

What happens after my 14-day free trial ends? +
Your login stays active after the trial. To keep full access to wallets, invoices, inventory, assets, and Bitcoin tools, continue with one of the plans above, or request an admin grant.
Is there a setup or installation fee? +
No. Monthly plans include 1 month free plus free installation, and yearly plans include 2 months free — 14 months of access in total — plus free installation on every currency.
Can I change my billing currency later? +
Yes — message the WhatsApp admin to switch your billing currency. Your workspace can still track wallets in AED, SAR, PKR, USD, and BTC regardless of which currency you're billed in.
Is my business data private from other accounts? +
Yes. Every account gets a completely isolated financial workspace, and every business plan runs on its own dedicated server for extra separation and security.
How do I pay, and can I cancel anytime? +
Reach out on WhatsApp to arrange payment for your region and activate your plan. There's no long-term lock-in beyond your chosen billing cycle — message the admin any time to change or cancel.
What are the official Triplem support channels? +
Only in-app chat support, WhatsApp at +92 333 900 4564, and the official Facebook page facebook.com/triplemvip are official Triplem channels. Never share your password or verification codes with anyone claiming to represent Triplem elsewhere.
