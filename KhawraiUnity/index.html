Choose a blood group to see its compatible red-cell donors or recipients.

Good to know

O- is the universal red-cell donor. It can donate red cells to every blood group.

AB+ can receive red cells from all groups. It is known as the universal recipient.

One donation can help up to three lives. Blood may be separated into useful components.

A healthy person may donate every 3–4 months. Always follow local blood-bank guidance.

This is a red-cell compatibility guide. A hospital must always confirm compatibility and cross-match blood before transfusion.

Get the App

Install the Khawrai Unity app for quick, on-the-go access to the donor directory.

Scan with your phone camera
  1. Tap Download for Android below, or scan the QR code with your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  4. Tap Install, then open the app once it finishes.
Download for Android

This app is distributed directly by Khawrai Unity, not the Play Store, so Android will show a one-time permission prompt before installing.

Scan with your iPhone camera
  1. Tap Download for iOS below, or scan the QR code with your iPhone.
  2. Open Settings and tap General than go to VPN and Device Management and select Khawrai Unity Profile near the top.
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, then confirm.
  4. Once installed, the Khawrai Unity app will be available from your Home Screen.
Download for iOS

This is a configuration profile provided directly by Khawrai Unity, not the App Store, so iOS will ask you to confirm the install in Settings.
