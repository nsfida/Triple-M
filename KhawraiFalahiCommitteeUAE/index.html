ⓘ About the Committee

The Khawrai Falahi Committee UAE was established in December 2016. The purpose of the committee is to address the issues faced by residents of Khawrai in the United Arab Emirates and its surroundings, and to resolve them in a timely and professional manner. The leadership of the committee is committed to serving the residents of Khawrai in the United Arab Emirates and remains determined to resolve their concerns with speed and responsibility.

✔ Mission

Residents of Khawrai village and the surrounding areas who live in the United Arab Emirates may face serious situations that are difficult to resolve alone. At times, unexpected emergencies arise where financial assistance, timely community support, and other essential resources become necessary, and these cannot be managed by a single person or a small group alone. With this need in mind, the Khawrai Falahi Committee was formed. The committee includes essential measures intended to support our community members in the UAE and provide timely solutions to their difficulties. The core mission of the committee focuses on three critical areas: a member suffering a heart attack, the passing of a member, and cases involving a member's appendicitis. Further details are available in the committee constitution.

⌯⌲ Vision

To build a digitally accessible, strong, and self-supporting Khawrai community in the UAE.
