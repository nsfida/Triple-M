President's Message

It is my honor to serve the Khawrai community in the United Arab Emirates. Our mission has always been to support, guide, and stand beside our people in every challenge they face. Together, we have built a strong and united community based on trust, respect, and service.

We remain committed to resolving issues efficiently and continuing our efforts for the welfare of our people. Your support and unity are the strength of this committee, and with your cooperation, we will continue to grow and serve better.
