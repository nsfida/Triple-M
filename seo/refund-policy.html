Our promise

A full refund within 14 days, no questions asked.

If you are not happy with Triplem VIP Pro, you can request a full refund within 14 days of the payment date. This applies to your first payment and to every renewal payment. You do not need to give a reason.

Card payments

Refunds are handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our card orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns for card orders.

  • Use the link in your Paddle receipt email, visit paddle.net to find your order, or email us at info@triplem.vip and we will arrange it.
  • The refund goes back to the card or payment method you used. Banks usually show it within 5–10 business days.
  • After a full refund, the Pro time added by that payment (including any bonus months it gave) is removed, and future renewals are cancelled.
Bank-transfer payments

Refunded by us to your bank account.

Email info@triplem.vip or message us on WhatsApp +92 333 9004564 within 14 days of the payment, from your registered account details. We refund the full amount by bank transfer to the account that paid, normally within 7 business days, and remove the Pro time that payment added.

After 14 days

Cancel any time, keep what you paid for.

After 14 days a payment is no longer refundable, but you can cancel at any time so it does not renew. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you paid for. If the law where you live gives you stronger refund rights, those rights apply.
