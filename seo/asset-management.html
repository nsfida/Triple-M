Accounting boundary

Asset Management is operational; formal Fixed Assets accounting remains separate.

The current separation avoids rewriting historical asset records into Accounting ERP without an explicit migration strategy.

Current behavior

Creating or depreciating an asset in Asset Management does not automatically post Fixed Assets, Accumulated Depreciation, Depreciation Expense, Cash or Bank journals in Accounting ERP. Formal accounts should be posted separately when required for financial statements.

Questions

Practical questions about asset management.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP workspace and the boundaries of this operational module.

What kinds of assets can I track?

Triplem VIP can be used for operational assets such as vehicles, property, equipment and investments, with purchase information, revenue, expenses, sale or disposal information and profitability views.

Does Triplem VIP support depreciation assets?

Yes. A separate Depreciation Assets workflow calculates monthly and annual depreciation, accumulated depreciation, book value and remaining useful life from the configured asset assumptions.

Can asset purchase cost be deducted from a wallet?

The current asset and depreciation-asset creation workflows can use a same-currency operational wallet deduction or skip the deduction when the user does not want to affect an operational wallet.

What PDF reports are available for assets?

Standard assets support portfolio and per-asset PDF reporting, while depreciation assets support overview, full schedule, yearly summary, valuation and, where applicable, disposal reporting.
