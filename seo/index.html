Security, notifications & support

Security and support travel with the Triplem VIP workflow.

The finance experience can be paired with Authenticator App 2FA, protected Smart PIN and account recovery, explicit Web Push consent, visitor Live Support and authenticated in-app support.

Authenticator 2FA

TOTP secrets are encrypted server-side and recovery codes are stored as one-way hashes.

Smart PIN protection

Username-bound hashing and server-managed lockout controls protect quick verification.

Visitor Live Support

Public visitors can begin with Aziz and request a human Agent through the supported Live Chat workflow.

In-app care

Registered businesses and individuals can use the authenticated support channel where enabled.
