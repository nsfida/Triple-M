Accounting ERP

Formal books beside day-to-day finance.

Triplem VIP adds a dedicated accounting layer for businesses that need balanced books, customer and supplier control, bank matching and period reporting without destroying their existing operational workflow.

The Accounting workspace treats the general ledger as the financial source of truth for records posted inside Accounting. Every posted journal must balance, and sales, purchase and payment workflows create ledger effects that can be reviewed later through the General Ledger and financial reports.

Existing Expenses, Wallets, Inventory, Assets and Loans remain separate operational modules in the current build. This protects historical data and lets administrators introduce formal accounting gradually instead of forcing every existing user into a migration of their daily records.

Typical workflow

From commercial document to financial statement.

The safest way to understand Accounting ERP is to follow one transaction all the way through the books.

01Create the partyAdd a customer or supplier with the identifying and tax information you need.
02Draft the documentPrepare a sales invoice, purchase bill or another supported document without affecting posted books yet.
03Issue and postPosting converts the approved document into formal accounting entries.
04Record settlementReceive customer money or pay the supplier against the open balance.
05Reconcile and reportMatch bank evidence and review the effect in ledger and financial reports.

Accounting controls

Questions

Practical questions about accounting erp.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

What is the Accounting ERP in Triplem VIP?

It is a dedicated double-entry accounting workspace with Chart of Accounts, journals, sales and purchase documents, customer and supplier balances, payments, bank reconciliation, tax analysis, dimensions, audit history and financial reports.

Does Accounting ERP replace the existing Expenses or Inventory sections?

No. The current Accounting workspace is intentionally separate from legacy operational modules so existing records and user workflows are not silently rewritten.

Can an administrator control access to Accounting?

Yes. The main administrator can enable or disable Accounting for individual users. When disabled, the Accounting tab is hidden and the related permissions are restricted.

Are posted journals editable?

Draft journals can be edited. Posted journals are treated as immutable accounting history and corrections use reversal-based voiding rather than silent rewriting.

Can Triplem VIP produce financial statements?

Yes. The current report set includes Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Cash Flow Activity, aging, party balances, registers, VAT Summary, reconciliation status and dimension profitability.
