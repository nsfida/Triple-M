Manual migration approach

If Inventory is currently maintained in Excel, first define the catalogue structure you want to keep, then create the corresponding categories, product records, customers and opening stock position in Triplem VIP. The objective is a controlled transition, not a blind copy of every spreadsheet column.

01Map the spreadsheetIdentify which sheets represent products, stock, customers, sales or balances.
02Create the catalogueSet up the categories, product lines and variants needed by the business.
03Establish current positionEnter the current stock and customer information you want to carry forward.
04Continue prospectivelyRecord new sales and stock movement in Triplem VIP from the chosen transition point.

Global currency support

International finance for supported currencies and regional workflows.

Triplem VIP currently supports AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription presentation can use a supported local billing currency.

Central currency formatting

Supported modules use the workspace currency configuration and dedicated symbol-font rules instead of hardcoding one dollar symbol.

Regional context

Users can operate the finance workspace internationally while tax, statutory and reporting obligations remain jurisdiction-specific.

Documents and reports

Where supported, invoices, receipts and reports follow the same configured currency presentation as the underlying workspace records.
