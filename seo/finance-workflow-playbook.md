## From daily money movement to reviewable books, reports and audit evidence

A product-specific operating playbook for keeping operational speed, formal accounting discipline and AI-assisted work inside clear financial boundaries.

## Use the right layer for the right financial question.

Triplem VIP deliberately separates operational finance from formal Accounting. Daily modules can capture expenses, receiving, transfers, inventory, loans, assets, installments and notes, while Accounting ERP provides double-entry books for transactions that must appear in formal statements.

### Operational capture

Record day-to-day activity quickly with the module that reflects what actually happened.

### Formal posting

Use Accounting workflows for journalized activity that must affect Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and other formal reports.

### Chronological context

Use Financial Timeline to review permitted activity across modules without treating that chronological view as a General Ledger.

## Let AI prepare work without silently changing the books.

Triplem AI can retrieve authorized workspace information and prepare supported finance Drafts. A Draft remains isolated from balances, VAT, stock, reports and Accounting until the user explicitly finalizes it.

- Verify cited source records before acting on an AI answer.
- Keep draft creation separate from financial finalization.
- Preserve module permissions and workspace boundaries.
- Treat AI assistance as review support, not autonomous bookkeeping.

## Finish with reconciliation, reporting and evidence.

A robust workflow ends by reconciling evidence, reviewing statements, investigating exceptions, exporting relevant records and preserving the context needed to explain how a figure was produced.
