Plans, billing and renewal

Clear prices, automatic renewal you control.

Pro is available monthly or yearly. The price, currency and any taxes are shown before you pay. Your first paid Pro plan may include bonus months as shown on the Plan & Subscription screen.

  • Card subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each paid period at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time from Account settings → Plan & Subscription → Manage billing, or from the link in your Paddle receipt. Cancelling stops future renewals; your access continues until the end of the period already paid.
  • If a renewal payment fails, Paddle may retry it. If it is not paid, Pro access ends when the paid period ends.
  • Bank-transfer payments extend access after we confirm the payment.
  • We may change prices for future periods; we will tell you before a change applies to your subscription.
