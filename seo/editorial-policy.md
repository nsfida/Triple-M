## Editorial policy for Triplem VIP public product and accounting guides

A transparent standard for product accuracy, update dates, authorship, jurisdiction boundaries, corrections and machine-readable public references.

## Product claims begin with the current Triplem VIP implementation.

Public guides describe the current product, its visible controls and its documented security or accounting boundaries. When a guide discusses jurisdiction-specific tax or compliance context, official authorities are preferred over secondary summaries.

- Do not invent features, customer statistics or regulatory approvals.
- Keep product boundaries explicit when operational modules differ from formal Accounting.
- Use visible update dates when a guide is materially revised.
- Keep canonical HTML and retrieval-oriented Markdown semantically aligned.

## Material errors should be corrected rather than preserved for marketing consistency.

If a product capability, price, currency, policy or regulatory reference changes, the public guide should be updated so users and retrieval systems do not continue receiving stale claims.

## Public guidance is educational product documentation, not jurisdiction-specific professional advice.

Triplem VIP can organize accounting and finance records, but users remain responsible for deciding how local law, tax rules, filing obligations and professional standards apply to their own circumstances.
