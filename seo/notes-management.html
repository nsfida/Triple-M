Why notes matter

Financial software still needs human context.

Notes are useful where the next decision cannot be represented by an amount alone.

Follow-up context

Remember a call, promise or next action related to a customer, supplier or internal matter.

Maintenance context

Keep asset or operational observations near the broader finance workspace.

Inventory context

Preserve non-transactional information about stock, sourcing or an expected action.

Personal workflow

Use scheduled reminders for tasks that should return to your attention later.

Accounting boundary

Notes inform decisions but do not post money.

Keeping notes separate prevents unstructured text from mutating financial statements.

Current behavior

Creating, editing or reminding a note does not create a journal, expense, invoice, asset transaction or any other accounting posting. Notes are contextual records only.

Questions

Practical questions about notes and reminders.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP workspace and the boundaries of this operational module.

What can I store in Notes?

Each note has a title and content so operational context, follow-ups, references and reminders can remain close to the rest of the workspace.

Can a note have a reminder?

Yes. Signed-in users can schedule note reminders, add reminder messages, reschedule them and manage due reminder alerts.

Can I search notes?

Yes. The Notes area supports searching so older operational context can be recovered without scanning every note manually.

Are Notes part of formal Accounting ERP?

No. Notes are an operational productivity module and do not create or alter double-entry accounting records.
