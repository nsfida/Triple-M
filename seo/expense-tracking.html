Everyday expense questions

The best tracker is the one that remains useful after months of records.

Structure should improve retrieval and understanding without making each entry unnecessarily difficult.

Should expenses be linked to a wallet or account?

Yes when possible. Wallet context makes balances, transfers and spending easier to reconcile because the record preserves where the money came from.

Why do categories and descriptions matter?

A bare amount tells very little later. Categories and descriptions make older spending searchable and explain the purpose of the transaction.

When is a simple expense app not enough?

When expenses need to coexist with invoices, stock, assets, loans, multiple currencies or customer documents, a broader finance workspace can reduce fragmentation.

Can expense tracking work for both personal and business records?

Yes. The same wallet and category model can support personal spending, while businesses can use additional modules around that core workflow.
