Expenses vs Accounting ERP

Operational record and formal ledger are different concepts.

The distinction protects legacy workflows while giving businesses a formal bookkeeping option.

Conceptual difference

AreaPrimary purposeAccounting effect
Expenses moduleOperational wallet and transaction managementDoes not automatically post into Accounting ERP in the current build
Accounting purchase / journalFormal bookkeeping and financial statementsCreates posted ledger activity when issued or posted

Use the workflow that matches the record you need. Formal financial reports are built from posted Accounting data.

Questions

Practical questions about expense tracking.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

Is the Expenses section the same as Accounting expense accounts?

No. The existing Expenses section is an operational wallet and transaction workflow. Accounting expense accounts belong to the formal double-entry ledger.

Will an expense entered in the old Expenses section automatically appear in Accounting reports?

Not in the current build. The two workflows remain separate to protect existing user data and behavior.

Can I still use Expenses without Accounting?

Yes. Accounting access is administrator-controlled and the existing Expenses workflow remains independent.

How should a business using formal Accounting record an expense?

Use the appropriate Accounting purchase or journal workflow for transactions that need to appear in formal financial reports, while operational Expenses can continue to serve its existing purpose.

Clear money views

Expenses, Receiving and Internal Transfers now answer different questions.

The Expenses section separates spending, incoming money and internal wallet movement so transfer legs do not pollute expense history.

Expenses

Shows pure expense records only. Add Money and wallet-to-wallet transfer activity are excluded from the Expenses view and expense-oriented totals.

Receiving

Shows money received through Add Money plus the incoming side of an internal wallet transfer. Module-specific receipts remain represented by their own modules where applicable.

Internal Transfers

Shows each wallet transfer once as the outgoing transfer record. The paired incoming ledger leg remains available for wallet balance integrity but is not shown as a second transfer entry.

Classification without data rewriting.

Existing transfer pairs remain intact for balance accuracy. Triplem VIP changes how they are classified and displayed rather than deleting historical transfer legs or pretending internal movement is operating income or expense.

Global currency support

International finance for India, Europe and users worldwide.

Triplem VIP is designed for international personal and business finance. Current supported currencies include AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription pricing can present a locally supported billing currency.

India with INR

Users in India can work with Indian Rupee (INR / ₹) across supported multi-currency finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

Euro-region support

Users in Belgium and other countries where the euro is used can work with EUR / € in supported finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

Need another currency?

Triplem VIP is built to serve users in any country. If your currency is not currently available, contact Triplem VIP and support can review adding it for your country.

Currency availability can expand as user demand grows. USD remains the regional default where another supported local billing currency does not apply. Tax, reporting and statutory requirements still depend on the user’s jurisdiction.
