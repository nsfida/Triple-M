Expenses vs Accounting ERP

Operational record and formal ledger are different concepts.

The distinction protects legacy workflows while giving businesses a formal bookkeeping option.

Conceptual difference

AreaPrimary purposeAccounting effect
Expenses moduleOperational wallet and transaction managementDoes not automatically post into Accounting ERP in the current build
Accounting purchase / journalFormal bookkeeping and financial statementsCreates posted ledger activity when issued or posted

Use the workflow that matches the record you need. Formal financial reports are built from posted Accounting data.

Questions

Practical questions about expense tracking.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

Is the Expenses section the same as Accounting expense accounts?

No. The existing Expenses section is an operational wallet and transaction workflow. Accounting expense accounts belong to the formal double-entry ledger.

Will an expense entered in the old Expenses section automatically appear in Accounting reports?

Not in the current build. The two workflows remain separate to protect existing user data and behavior.

Can I still use Expenses without Accounting?

Yes. Accounting access is administrator-controlled and the existing Expenses workflow remains independent.

How should a business using formal Accounting record an expense?

Use the appropriate Accounting purchase or journal workflow for transactions that need to appear in formal financial reports, while operational Expenses can continue to serve its existing purpose.
