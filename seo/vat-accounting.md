## Tax belongs to the commercial line that generated it.

Sales and purchase documents can carry tax codes on their individual lines.

## A management estimate, not an electronic filing.

Triplem VIP can calculate a configurable estimate from accounting profit, threshold and rate, but it deliberately labels the result as a management estimate.

The Corporate Tax figure is not a tax return, tax authority integration, legal determination or substitute for professional tax advice. Accounting profit can differ from taxable profit because tax law may require adjustments that are outside the current automated estimate.

## Use formal Accounting VAT and cross-module audit evidence for different control purposes.

Accounting VAT belongs to the formal ledger. The Financial Audit & Control Report can additionally inspect selected operational activity, but the two should not be blindly added when they represent the same transaction.

### Accounting VAT

Use tax codes and posted Accounting documents for formal Input Tax, Output Tax and Net VAT analysis.

### Audit evidence

Use the Financial Audit report to review permitted modules, source references and VAT-related classifications across a chosen period.

### Submission review

Triplem VIP prepares evidence and reports; it does not electronically file the VAT return or replace verification of tax treatment and source documents.
