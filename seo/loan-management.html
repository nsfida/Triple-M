Loan management

Track money lent, borrowed and repaid without losing the story behind the balance.

The Loan workspace is designed for practical obligations between the business or individual and another person or party.

Loans given

Record principal advanced to another party with person, amount, currency, date and notes.

Loans taken

Maintain borrowed amounts and the party from whom the money was received.

Repayments

Add linked repayment or return activity while preserving the original loan relationship.

Wallet context

Where selected, validate and connect operational wallet movement to the loan event.

History

Keep principal and payment history grouped for later review.

Controlled deletion

Use the project’s recycle-bin-oriented record handling instead of losing context casually.

Practical workflow

A loan should remain understandable from principal to final settlement.

The normal workflow keeps the original obligation and every linked payment together.

01Create the loanChoose whether money was given or taken, enter the party, principal, currency, date and notes.
02Connect wallet movementWhen applicable, choose the relevant operational wallet so the principal movement is reflected consistently.
03Record repaymentsAdd money returned or paid back against the original loan instead of creating disconnected transactions.
04Review positionUse the grouped history to understand the original principal and subsequent activity.
05Close the relationshipOnce fully settled, retain the history as evidence of how the obligation was resolved.

Accounting boundary

Operational loan history and formal accounting are currently separate.

This distinction protects existing user data and established workflows.

Current behavior

A loan recorded in the operational Loan section does not automatically create a Loans Payable, Receivable, Cash or Bank journal in Accounting ERP. If the transaction must appear in formal financial statements, record the appropriate Accounting entry separately until a native bridge is introduced.

Questions

Practical questions about loan management.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP workspace and the boundaries of this operational module.

What loan records can Triplem VIP manage?

Triplem VIP supports operational records for loans given and loans taken, together with repayments, return payments, dates, currency context, notes and linked history.

Can a loan be connected to a wallet?

Where the loan workflow uses a linked operational wallet, Triplem VIP validates the wallet and can reflect the principal movement in that wallet instead of leaving the cash movement disconnected.

Does Loan Management automatically post to Accounting ERP?

No. The operational Loan module remains separate from the formal Accounting ERP in the current build so existing loan history is not silently rewritten into journal entries.

Can I keep repayment history?

Yes. Loan principal and linked repayment activity remain grouped so the outstanding position and settlement history can be reviewed over time.

Faster loan workflows

Desktop shortcuts and AI Drafts reduce repetitive entry without bypassing controls.

Given / Received Back

Ctrl+Alt+G opens Loan Given, Ctrl+Alt+R opens Received Back, Ctrl+Alt+O filters Open/Partial and Ctrl+Alt+F focuses Search in this loan mode.

Taken / Returned

Ctrl+Alt+T opens Loan Taken, Ctrl+Alt+B opens Loan Returned, with the same Open/Partial and Search shortcuts scoped to this mode.

AI-assisted loan Drafts

Triplem AI can prepare supported loan records from natural language, but the Draft remains outside live balances until the user reviews and finalizes it.
