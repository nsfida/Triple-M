Two finance layers

Operational finance and formal accounting can coexist.

Triplem VIP now spans daily money control and double-entry accounting without pretending they are already one automatic posting engine.

The wider Triplem VIP workspace handles operational records that owners use throughout the day: expenses, wallets, inventory, assets, loans, installments, notes and other finance-related activity.

The Accounting workspace is the formal book: Chart of Accounts, journals, customers, suppliers, sales and purchase posting, receivables, payables, reconciliation, tax analysis and financial reports. The current build keeps these layers separate to protect legacy data.

Current product boundary

One workspace does not mean every module silently posts into Accounting.

The present architecture favors data safety over hidden automation.

Important current behavior

Operational records do not automatically become Accounting journals in the current build. Businesses adopting formal Accounting should establish opening balances and use Accounting workflows for transactions that must appear in formal financial statements. This boundary can support future integrations without corrupting existing user history.

Questions

Practical questions about finance software.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

What does Triplem VIP manage outside Accounting ERP?

The wider workspace includes operational Expenses and Wallets, Inventory, Assets, Loans, Installments, Notes, Bitcoin-oriented tools and support features.

Is Triplem VIP now an ERP?

The product contains a dedicated Accounting ERP workspace for formal books, while several operational modules remain independent rather than being automatically journal-integrated in the current build.

Why keep operational modules separate?

The separation protects existing user data and established workflows while formal Accounting can be introduced gradually.

Can Accounting be hidden from users who do not need it?

Yes. The main administrator can turn Accounting access on or off per user.

Global currency support

International finance for India, Europe and users worldwide.

Triplem VIP is designed for international personal and business finance. Current supported currencies include AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription pricing can present a locally supported billing currency.

India with INR

Users in India can work with Indian Rupee (INR / ₹) across supported multi-currency finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

Euro-region support

Users in Belgium and other countries where the euro is used can work with EUR / € in supported finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

Need another currency?

Triplem VIP is built to serve users in any country. If your currency is not currently available, contact Triplem VIP and support can review adding it for your country.

Currency availability can expand as user demand grows. USD remains the regional default where another supported local billing currency does not apply. Tax, reporting and statutory requirements still depend on the user’s jurisdiction.
