How to read the reports

Use reports together, not in isolation.

A report becomes more trustworthy when you can move from the summary back to the ledger logic that created it.

01Start with Trial BalanceConfirm the ledger structure and movements look sensible.
02Read P&LUnderstand revenue, expenses and period profitability.
03Read Balance SheetReview what the business owns, owes and retains.
04Investigate General LedgerTrace unusual balances back to posted entries.
05Review aging and reconciliationFollow outstanding parties and unexplained bank items before closing the period.

Questions

Practical questions about financial reports.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

Which financial reports are included?

The current report selector includes Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger, Cash Flow Activity, receivables aging, payables aging, customer balances, supplier balances, dimension profitability, bank reconciliation status, sales register, purchase register and VAT Summary.

Can reports be exported?

Yes. Accounting reports can be exported to PDF and CSV.

Are reports based on draft journals?

Core ledger reports use posted journal lines rather than unposted journal drafts.

Can I choose a reporting period?

The Accounting report toolbar supports From and To dates for the selected report.

Financial Audit & Control Report

Timeline vs reports
