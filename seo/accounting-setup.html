Before setup

Choose a transition point instead of rebuilding history blindly.

Existing Triplem VIP operational data is deliberately not auto-converted into Accounting journals. For an established business, begin formal Accounting from a date you can reconcile confidently.

Recommended transition approach

Take a reliable closing position from the business records you already trust, choose the date formal Accounting begins, create balanced opening balances, and then post future Accounting activity through the new workspace. This preserves legacy operational history while giving formal reports a clean starting point.

Setup sequence

A deliberate setup order reduces corrections later.

The sequence below moves from access and settings into live posting, reconciliation and period control.

01Enable Accounting accessThe main administrator enables Accounting for the required user.
02Configure base booksSet base currency, fiscal start, invoice terms and tax registration information.
03Review Chart of AccountsUse the protected defaults and add only the accounts needed for meaningful reporting.
04Create contacts and dimensionsAdd customers, suppliers, projects, branches or cost centres as required.
05Establish opening balancesFor an existing business, post a balanced opening journal on the chosen transition date.
01Begin sales and purchase postingCreate documents as drafts, review them, then issue and post approved transactions.
02Record paymentsApply customer receipts and supplier payments against open documents.
03Reconcile bank activityImport or enter statement rows and match them to posted journals.
04Review financial reportsRun Trial Balance, P&L, Balance Sheet, General Ledger and aging reports.
05Lock finalized periodsAfter review, use Books Lock Date to prevent new backdated posting into the closed period.

Questions

Practical questions about accounting setup.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

What should I configure first?

Start with Accounting access, base currency, fiscal settings and the Chart of Accounts before posting live transactions.

How should an existing business begin without rewriting old data?

Choose a transition date, calculate opening balances from the records you trust, post a balanced opening journal, then use Accounting prospectively for transactions that need to appear in formal reports.

Should I enable Accounting for every team member?

Not necessarily. The main administrator can enable Accounting only for users whose role requires access.

What is Books Lock Date for?

It blocks posting on or before a finalized date and is controlled by the company owner.
