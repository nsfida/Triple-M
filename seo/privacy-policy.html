Who we are

Triplem VIP is responsible for the information described here.

Triplem VIP is a private accounting and business finance workspace available at https://triplem.vip/, as an installable web app, on mobile and as a Windows app (direct download and Microsoft Store). It is created and operated by Nadeem Shahzad Fida.

Questions or requests about privacy: info@triplem.vip. This policy is effective from 2026-11-04.

Information we collect

Only what is needed to run your workspace and keep it secure.

We collect the information you or your organisation administrator enter, plus limited technical data needed to operate the service.

  • Account details: name, username, email address and, if provided, phone number, profile photo and business details.
  • Records you create: accounting entries, expenses, income, invoices, inventory, loans, installment plans, insurance, assets, notes, attachments and photos.
  • Security data: passwords are stored only as salted hashes; two-factor (Authenticator) settings, passkeys, Smart PIN settings, sign-in sessions and security events such as failed sign-in attempts.
  • Support conversations you start with Triplem VIP live support or the in-app assistant.
  • Anonymous website statistics: pages viewed, referring page, device type, browser, operating system, campaign tags and visit duration, linked to a random visitor ID stored in your browser. No advertising cookies or cross-site trackers are used.
  • Push-notification subscription, only if you enable notifications on a device.
How we use information

To provide the service you asked for, never to sell or advertise.

Information is used to provide and secure your workspace, show your reports, sync your devices, answer support requests, prevent abuse and improve reliability.

  • We do not sell personal or financial information.
  • We do not use your records for advertising or share them with advertisers.
  • Records are isolated per account and organisation by database access rules; other customers cannot see them.
Service providers

Trusted processors that run parts of the service on our behalf.

We use a small number of providers who process data only to deliver the service:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and server functions that hold your account and records.
  • Google Gemini API: when you use AI assistant features, your question and the record context needed to answer it are sent to Google to generate the reply.
  • Your browser or operating system push service (for example Google, Apple, Microsoft or Mozilla) delivers notifications you enabled.
  • Website hosting and content delivery networks serve the public website files, fonts and icons.
  • Microsoft Store distributes the Windows app; Microsoft handles Store accounts, downloads and payments under its own privacy statement.
Data stored on your device

Some data stays on your device so the app is fast and works offline.

To work offline, recent records and pending changes are kept in your browser or app storage on the device and synchronised when you are back online. Signing out clears the session from the device.

The Windows app stores its browser data and a diagnostic log in your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Triplem VIP). Uninstalling the app removes it.

Bitcoin private keys used in wallet features are handled in your browser and are not sent to Triplem VIP servers.

Retention and deletion

You stay in control of your records.

Records are kept while your account is active so your history and reports remain complete. You can export and back up your data from the app at any time.

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and records by emailing info@triplem.vip from your registered email address. We delete account data within 30 days of a verified request, except where we must keep limited information to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Anonymous website statistics are not linked to your identity.

Your rights

Access, correction, export and deletion.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data and to object to certain processing. Contact info@triplem.vip and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Protection designed in, described in detail on our Security page.

Connections use HTTPS encryption. Access is protected by hashed passwords, optional Authenticator two-factor sign-in and passkeys, session controls and database row-level security. See https://triplem.vip/Security/ for details.

Children

Triplem VIP is a business tool.

The service is intended for adults and businesses and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date when this policy changes.

If we make material changes we will update this page and its effective date, and notify signed-in users where appropriate.
