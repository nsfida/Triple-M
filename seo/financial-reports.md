## Financial reports built from posted accounting books

Move from Trial Balance and P&L to detailed General Ledger, aging, reconciliation, VAT and management analysis, with PDF and CSV exports.

## Translate posted ledger activity into financial meaning.

The central report set covers balances, performance and detailed ledger evidence.

### Trial Balance

Period debit, period credit and closing balance for accounts with activity or balances.

### Profit & Loss

Income, expenses and calculated net profit or loss for the selected period.

### Balance Sheet

Assets, liabilities, equity and current unclosed earnings as of the selected date.

### General Ledger

Every posted journal line with date, journal number, account, description, debit and credit.

### Cash Flow Activity

Monthly inflows, outflows and net movement for accounts marked as cash accounts.

### PDF & CSV

Download the current report for sharing, review or further analysis.

## Follow customers, suppliers and outstanding balances.

Receivables and payables reports focus on open commercial documents rather than only high-level control-account balances.

### Receivables Aging

Open customer documents grouped into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day buckets.

### Payables Aging

Open supplier documents grouped into the same aging buckets.

### Customer Balances

Customer-level invoice counts and open balances.

### Supplier Balances

Supplier-level bill counts and open balances.

### Sales Register

Date, document, customer, type, net, tax, total and status.

### Purchase Register

Date, document, supplier, type, net, tax, total and status.

## Chronology answers when. Reports answer financial position and performance.

Financial Timeline complements reporting but does not replace Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger or the Financial Audit & Control Report.

### Financial Timeline

Read-only chronological activity grouped by date across permitted modules, with currency-aware inflow, outflow and net summaries.

### Accounting reports

Formal statements derive from posted Accounting records and preserve double-entry reporting logic.

### Financial Audit

Cross-module control reporting classifies operational money movement, non-cash activity and Accounting evidence for review.

## International finance for India, Europe and users worldwide.

Triplem VIP is designed for international personal and business finance. Current supported currencies include AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription pricing can present a locally supported billing currency.

### India with INR

Users in India can work with Indian Rupee (INR / ₹) across supported multi-currency finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

### Euro-region support

Users in Belgium and other countries where the euro is used can work with EUR / € in supported finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

### Need another currency?

Triplem VIP is built to serve users in any country. If your currency is not currently available, contact Triplem VIP and support can review adding it for your country.

Currency availability can expand as user demand grows. USD remains the regional default where another supported local billing currency does not apply. Tax, reporting and statutory requirements still depend on the user’s jurisdiction.
