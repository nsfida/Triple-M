Questions

Practical questions about what is triplem vip?.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

What is Triplem VIP?

Triplem VIP is a browser-based private accounting and finance workspace with a dedicated double-entry Accounting ERP plus operational modules for expenses, wallets, inventory, assets, loans and other business records.

Who is Accounting ERP for?

It is intended for users who need formal bookkeeping, customer and supplier balances, reconciliation, tax analysis and financial statements. Main Admin controls access per user.

Can individuals use Triplem VIP without formal Accounting?

Yes. Operational modules can be used independently where Accounting is not needed or not enabled.

Does Triplem VIP automatically merge old operational records into Accounting?

No. The current Accounting workspace remains separate to protect existing data and workflows.
