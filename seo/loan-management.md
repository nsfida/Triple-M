## Operational loan history and formal accounting are currently separate.

This distinction protects existing user data and established workflows.

A loan recorded in the operational Loan section does not automatically create a Loans Payable, Receivable, Cash or Bank journal in Accounting ERP. If the transaction must appear in formal financial statements, record the appropriate Accounting entry separately until a native bridge is introduced.

## Desktop shortcuts and AI Drafts reduce repetitive entry without bypassing controls.

### Given / Received Back

Ctrl+Alt+G opens Loan Given, Ctrl+Alt+R opens Received Back, Ctrl+Alt+O filters Open/Partial and Ctrl+Alt+F focuses Search in this loan mode.

### Taken / Returned

Ctrl+Alt+T opens Loan Taken, Ctrl+Alt+B opens Loan Returned, with the same Open/Partial and Search shortcuts scoped to this mode.

### AI-assisted loan Drafts

Triplem AI can prepare supported loan records from natural language, but the Draft remains outside live balances until the user reviews and finalizes it.
