## Build a cleaner VAT review before submission.

The audit report can organize VAT-related evidence, but it is not an automatic tax filing service or a substitute for professional tax review.

The executive summary separates VAT Collected and VAT Paid and can calculate a positive payable position or a negative input-tax credit position for the selected operational scope. Formal Accounting VAT remains separately classified to reduce the risk of double counting the same economic activity.

This makes the report useful as a pre-submission control workbook: review the selected period, isolate the correct modules, inspect references and details, resolve exceptions, then export the evidence for accountant or internal review.

Triplem VIP can prepare and export VAT-oriented audit evidence. The user remains responsible for verifying the applicable tax period, tax treatment, source documents and final figures before submitting a VAT return to the relevant authority.

## Important distinctions before using an audit export for VAT review.

### Is this an electronic VAT return?

No. The report organizes financial and VAT-oriented evidence for review and export; it does not submit a tax return to a government authority.

### Why are internal transfers separated?

Moving money between owned wallets is not ordinary revenue or operating expense, so the audit classifies internal movement separately from business Money In and Money Out.

### Are multiple currencies silently converted?

No. The audit keeps currencies separate unless a deliberate single-currency scope is selected, avoiding invented exchange-rate assumptions in a control report.

### Why is Accounting VAT separate?

Formal Accounting entries may mirror operational activity. Keeping Accounting VAT separate helps reviewers avoid adding the same economic transaction twice without an explicit reconciliation rule.
