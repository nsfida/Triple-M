# Excel to Triplem VIP | Custom Finance Setup & Migration Guide

> Move spreadsheet-led finance into Triplem VIP: map Excel workflows, enter opening positions, configure the modules you need and continue from a controlled transition date.

Reviewed: 2026-09-21

## Move the workflow, not spreadsheet clutter

Triplem VIP is modular. Users can manually recreate the finance and operational setup they actually need instead of copying every worksheet, formula, color and unused column.

Triplem VIP does not claim a universal one-click importer for every possible Excel workbook. The transition is deliberate: map the current workflow, choose the relevant modules, carry forward verified opening positions and continue future work from a controlled date.

## Common mappings

- Cash, bank and daily spending → Wallets and Expenses.
- Stock, customers and sales → Inventory.
- Insurance providers, policies and commission tracking → Insurance.
- Loan schedules → Loans.
- Fixed assets and depreciation → Assets.
- Payment schedules → Installment Plans Bought or Sold.
- Follow-up sheets → Notes and reminders.
- Formal books → Accounting ERP.

## Opening positions

Not every historical row has to be recreated. Depending on the module, a user can establish a current stock position, carry forward relevant outstanding obligations, create a balanced Accounting opening journal, or enter an Insurance commission opening balance already owed before Triplem VIP.

## Customize the workspace

Users can build different setups with module permissions, categories, products, accounts, customers, Insurance Companies, policy types, referrers, supported currencies, themes and document workflows. A personal user and a multi-user company do not need identical configurations.

## A practical migration sequence

1. List the spreadsheets and understand what each one tracks.
2. Choose the matching Triplem VIP modules.
3. Create reusable master records.
4. Enter verified opening positions.
5. Test representative transactions.
6. Begin ongoing work from a controlled transition date.

Old spreadsheets can remain as historical evidence while Triplem VIP becomes the prospective workspace.
