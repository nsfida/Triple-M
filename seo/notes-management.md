## Searchable business notes with reminders built into the finance workspace

Keep titles, operational context and future follow-ups together, then surface important notes again with scheduled reminders instead of relying on memory or disconnected apps.

## Keep the explanation beside the numbers.

Not every business fact belongs in a journal or transaction description. Notes preserve the context that helps a future user understand what needs attention.

### Clear titles

Give each note a concise title so it remains identifiable in the sticky-note grid.

### Free-form content

Store operational explanations, follow-ups, references or working information.

### Search

Filter notes by relevant text instead of manually opening every record.

### Edit

Update note content when the underlying situation changes.

### Delete

Remove obsolete notes through the protected note workflow.

### Sticky-note presentation

Use a compact note-oriented visual treatment instead of presenting notes like financial ledger rows.

## Turn a note into a future action.

A note can carry a scheduled reminder so operational context resurfaces when it is actually needed.

## Financial software still needs human context.

Notes are useful where the next decision cannot be represented by an amount alone.

### Follow-up context

Remember a call, promise or next action related to a customer, supplier or internal matter.

### Maintenance context

Keep asset or operational observations near the broader finance workspace.

### Inventory context

Preserve non-transactional information about stock, sourcing or an expected action.

### Personal workflow

Use scheduled reminders for tasks that should return to your attention later.

## Notes inform decisions but do not post money.

Keeping notes separate prevents unstructured text from mutating financial statements.

Creating, editing or reminding a note does not create a journal, expense, invoice, asset transaction or any other accounting posting. Notes are contextual records only.

## Create, search and prepare notes with less friction.

### Ctrl+Alt+N

Creates a New Note only while Notes is the active desktop section.

### Ctrl+Alt+F

Focuses the Notes search while Notes is active.

### Triplem AI note Draft

Ask Triplem AI to prepare a note from natural language, review it, then finalize it into the private Notes workspace.

## International finance for India, Europe and users worldwide.

Triplem VIP is designed for international personal and business finance. Current supported currencies include AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription pricing can present a locally supported billing currency.

### India with INR

Users in India can work with Indian Rupee (INR / ₹) across supported multi-currency finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

### Euro-region support

Users in Belgium and other countries where the euro is used can work with EUR / € in supported finance workflows and regional subscription pricing.

### Need another currency?

Triplem VIP is built to serve users in any country. If your currency is not currently available, contact Triplem VIP and support can review adding it for your country.

Currency availability can expand as user demand grows. USD remains the regional default where another supported local billing currency does not apply. Tax, reporting and statutory requirements still depend on the user’s jurisdiction.
