Questions

Practical questions about receivables & payables.

The answers below describe the current Triplem VIP Accounting workspace and its present boundaries.

What are accounts receivable?

Accounts receivable represent amounts customers still owe on posted receivable documents.

What are accounts payable?

Accounts payable represent amounts the business still owes suppliers on posted payable documents.

Can partial payments be recorded?

Yes. Payments can be recorded against open documents, reducing the remaining balance until it reaches zero.

Does Triplem VIP provide aging reports?

Yes. Receivables and payables aging reports group open balances into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day buckets based on due dates.

Operational installment boundary

Installment Plans Sold and Accounting Receivables are related ideas, not the same ledger.

Triplem VIP keeps the operational installment schedule distinct from formal Accounts Receivable unless the user records the corresponding Accounting document and payment workflow.

Why the distinction matters

A Sold installment plan can show who is expected to pay, advances received, installment receipts and the remaining operational receivable. Accounting AR/AP remains document- and posting-driven for formal books, aging and financial statements.
