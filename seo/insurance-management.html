Insurance workflow

Manage insurance providers, reusable policies, sales and customer documents in one dedicated workspace.

Insurance is separate from Inventory and formal Accounting. Providers and policy types are maintained independently so the same policy can be sold through different insurance companies.

Insurance companies

Maintain the providers your business works with, including status, contact context and notes, without tying a policy permanently to one company.

Reusable policy catalogue

Create policy types once, such as comprehensive or third-party cover, then select any active policy with any active provider when making a sale.

Customers

Use walk-in customers, existing customers or create a new customer within the sale flow while preserving persistent customer identity.

Customer documents

Generate Invoice, Receipt and editable temporary Invoice documents using the workspace identity, customer details, policy number and transaction information.

Reports

Filter insurance sales by date, provider, policy and customer, then review commission-focused or full financial views.

Controlled access

Insurance is an administrator-controlled module and follows the workspace and team permission boundary rather than relying on a hidden navigation card alone.

Sale pricing & commission

Keep gross premium, provider cost, customer selling price and earned commission mathematically distinct.

Insurance pricing is entered when the policy is sold because the same policy can have different provider costs and customer prices across transactions.

Gross Premium

The insurer's normal premium or reference value for the transaction.

Purchase Price

What the Insurance Company charges your business for that policy.

Sale Price

What your business actually charges the customer.

Company Commission

Gross Premium minus Purchase Price: the original commission available before a customer discount.

Customer Discount

Gross Premium minus Sale Price: the amount of the reference premium passed back to the customer.

My Commission

Sale Price minus Purchase Price: the actual earned commission or loss for the sale.

My Commission, cancellations & opening balances

Track what each Insurance Company still owes without rewriting the original sale.

Commission receivables are settled through their own ledger so partial payments, opening balances, cancellation deductions and receipt history remain auditable.

Opening outstanding balance

Enter commission already owed before moving to Triplem VIP, with company, date and explanatory note, without creating fake historical sales.

Partial or combined receipts

Record one company payment against one or several selected commissions. Fully covered items close while any remainder stays outstanding.

Policy cancellation

Record cancellation date, policy usage duration, reason and commission deduction while preserving the original policy sale.

Commission deductions

Post a cancellation deduction against the same sale's commission receivable or retain it for later company settlement.

Payment history

Keep receipt, allocation and deduction history with date, time, reference and notes.

Top totals

Review Total Commission, Total Commission Received and Balance to Receive, including imported opening balances.

Referral commissions

Keep referral payables separate from the commission the Insurance Company owes your business.

A referral can be selected from the existing referral directory or created during the sale. Referral commission is tracked as a separate payable and never reduces the company-facing My Commission receivable.

Reusable referral directory

Choose an existing referrer instead of retyping the name for every policy, or add a new referrer in the same compact sale workflow.

Grouped referrer ledger

Each referrer appears once in the main list. Open that person to see every linked policy transaction and commission.

Partial payments

Pay referral commission fully or partially while keeping the remaining amount outstanding.

Policy-level detail

See provider, policy, policy number, sale reference, customer, date, commission, paid amount and outstanding balance per referral transaction.

Policy number, documents & reporting

Carry policy identity into customer-facing documents while keeping internal margins private.

The policy number is stored with the sale and appears on Insurance Invoice, Receipt and editable temporary Invoice documents. Internal purchase cost and commission data remain separate from customer-facing documents.

Document boundary

Customer documents show customer-relevant policy and sale information. Internal Purchase Price, My Commission, company settlement history and referral payable details are not exposed merely because they exist in the internal Insurance ledger.

Global currency support

International finance for supported currencies and regional workflows.

Triplem VIP currently supports AED, SAR, PKR, USD, EUR, INR and BTC where applicable, while regional subscription presentation can use a supported local billing currency.

Central currency formatting

Supported modules use the workspace currency configuration and dedicated symbol-font rules instead of hardcoding one dollar symbol.

Regional context

Users can operate the finance workspace internationally while tax, statutory and reporting obligations remain jurisdiction-specific.

Documents and reports

Where supported, invoices, receipts and reports follow the same configured currency presentation as the underlying workspace records.
