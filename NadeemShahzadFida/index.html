My Approach to Modern Development

Think · Prompt · Build · Test · Refine

My approach to modern development is strongly AI-assisted and iteration-driven.

I use AI as a development multiplier rather than as a replacement for product thinking. My process begins with the problem I want to solve. I describe the business context, the user journey, the information that must be stored, the decisions the interface should support and the edge cases that could cause problems. I then use AI to accelerate exploration, implementation, debugging, refactoring, documentation and repetitive development work while I remain responsible for the final product direction.

I call this style of working vibe coding because I can move rapidly between an idea, a visual concept, a working prototype and a refined feature. However, I do not treat generated code as automatically correct. I inspect the result, test it, challenge assumptions, look for edge cases, correct the implementation and iterate until the behavior matches the actual requirement.

  • I start with the problem: I define what the user needs to accomplish before deciding how the code should be written.
  • I translate business logic into software: I turn accounting, finance and operational rules into screens, records, calculations and workflows.
  • I prototype quickly: I use AI to explore layouts, interactions, components and implementation alternatives without spending unnecessary time on boilerplate.
  • I stay hands-on: I read, test and modify the generated implementation instead of blindly accepting it.
  • I iterate visually: I refine spacing, hierarchy, responsive behavior, tables, forms, dashboards, exports and print layouts based on the actual result.
  • I debug systematically: I isolate failures, explain the desired behavior, test fixes and verify that changes do not break other workflows.
  • I think about data: I consider validation, consistency, relationships, backups, exports, access boundaries and the consequences of incorrect records.
  • I think about security: I pay attention to authentication, workspace isolation, sensitive information, client-side key handling and safe user workflows.
  • I build for real use: I care about what happens when a user enters incomplete information, edits an old record, prints a report or encounters an unexpected situation.

My Development Loop

Understand

I clarify the problem, users, business rules, desired output and constraints.

Design

I decide the information architecture, interface hierarchy, data flow and user journey.

Generate

I use AI-assisted coding to accelerate the first implementation and explore practical alternatives.

Test

I run the feature, inspect the behavior, test edge cases and compare the result with the requirement.

Refine

I improve usability, correctness, responsiveness, performance, visual hierarchy and maintainability.

Ship

I turn the refined result into a practical product experience and continue improving it through feedback.

What Vibe Coding Means to Me

For me, vibe coding is not simply asking an AI to write a large amount of code. It is a modern way of collaborating with an AI system while I provide the product judgment, context, constraints and quality expectations. It allows me to move from concept to usable software much faster, but the responsibility for deciding whether the software is actually useful, correct and appropriate remains with me.

Neerang-e-Hikmat

Neerang-e-Hikmat is my published Urdu work in which Islamic wisdom, philosophy, spirituality and reflective thought meet in a single literary journey. At its heart is a sustained contemplation of the Oneness of Allah, the mysteries of the universe, the relationship between creation and the Creator, the purpose of human existence and the soul's deeper connection with its Divine source.

I wrote the book not merely to present information, but to invite the reader into reflection. Its approach moves between faith, intellect, intuition, metaphysical questions, love, existence and the meaning of human life, while using eloquent Urdu prose and thought-provoking poetry to give the ideas both intellectual and emotional dimensions.

Divine Oneness: The book explores the intellectual and reflective dimensions of Tawhid and considers what Divine Oneness means for our understanding of existence, creation and the human condition.

Universe & Existence: It reflects upon the mysteries of the cosmos through the relationship between intellect, intuition, creation and the Creator, encouraging the reader to look beyond the merely visible.

Creation & Human Purpose: The work asks deeper questions about why human beings exist, what gives life meaning and how the soul relates to its origin and ultimate purpose.

Ishq & Spirituality: The concept of Ishq is used to explore the distinction between worldly attachment and deeper, more enduring love, giving spiritual reflection a distinctly human dimension.

Faith & Reflection: Rather than treating faith and thought as isolated domains, the book encourages contemplation in which reason, intuition, spirituality and literary expression can work together.
