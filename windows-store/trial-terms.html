Free Trial Terms and Conditions

Version 2026-11-11.2, effective 11 November 2026. These terms apply to the free trial of every new Triplem VIP account, including accounts created in the Windows app from the Microsoft Store.

Summary

  • Your free trial lasts 14 days from the moment your account is created. It is free: no card, no payment details and no automatic charge.
  • During the trial you have full access to the workspace modules you chose at sign-up.
  • The trial never turns into a paid plan by itself. You pay only if you choose a Pro plan and send the payment yourself.
  • When the trial ends you keep full access for a 3-day grace period. The app shows the exact date and time it ends.
  • After the grace period your account moves to limited access: it stays active, you can still sign in, view and download all of your records, but new entries, changes and deletions are paused until a Pro plan is approved.
  • Your account is not switched off and your records are not deleted because a trial or plan has ended.
  • A banner in your account counts down the remaining days from the first day, and Account Settings › Plan & Subscription shows every date.

1. About these Free Trial Terms

These Free Trial Terms (version 2026-11-11.2, effective 11 November 2026) explain how the free trial of Triplem VIP works. Triplem VIP is operated by Nadeem Shahzad Fida ("Triplem VIP", "we", "us"). Contact: info@triplem.vip, or Live Support on https://triplem.vip/ and in the app.

They apply together with the Triplem VIP Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy. If they differ, these Free Trial Terms apply to the free trial. You accept them when you create your account; the version and the date you accepted are recorded with your account.

  • "Trial period" means the 14 days that start when your account is created.
  • "Grace period" means the 3 days that follow the end of a trial period or of a paid plan, with full access.
  • "Limited access" means the state after the grace period, described in section 9.
  • "Records" means everything you or your team enter or upload: entries, documents, attachments, notes and settings.
  • "Pro plan" means Pro Monthly or Pro Yearly, with optional team member seats.

2. Who can use the free trial

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding agreement, for yourself or for the business you represent.
  • The free trial is for new accounts: one trial per person and per business. Opening several accounts to repeat the trial is not allowed.
  • The details you give at sign-up (name, email address, mobile number and, for company accounts, company name and address) must be true, complete and yours, and you must keep them up to date.
  • Sign-ups are limited per network to prevent abuse. If you see "too many sign-ups", please try again later or contact support.

3. Starting the trial

  • You choose the modules your workspace starts with. Selected by default: Expenses & Wallets, Inventory 2.0, Assets, Loans, Installments and Notes. Accounting is optional. Dashboard, Reports, PDF export and Currency settings are always included.
  • New accounts use Inventory 2.0. The Classic inventory, the Insurance module and Triplem AI are enabled by Triplem VIP on request.
  • You choose your workspace currencies; your billing currency follows your region.
  • Company accounts can add team members. During the trial, team members are included at no charge; after the trial, team seats are paid per seat.
  • To add or remove modules later, contact Triplem VIP support. Changing modules never deletes records.

4. Length and cost

  • The trial starts when your account is created and ends exactly 14 days later, at the same time of day.
  • The trial is free. We do not ask for a card or bank details, and nothing is charged automatically, during or after the trial.
  • The trial is not renewed or extended automatically. Triplem VIP may extend a trial on request; an extension is shown in your account with its new end date.

5. What the trial includes

  • Full use of every module you selected: add, edit, archive, import, export and report.
  • Exports and documents: reports, statements, PDF, CSV and Excel exports, receipts and invoices.
  • Account backups, offline use of recently opened records with automatic sync, and account security features (two-factor sign-in, passkeys, Smart PIN, trusted devices).
  • Live Support with the AI assistant and, during support hours, the Triplem VIP team.
  • The website, the installable web app, the mobile apps and the Windows app all use the same account and the same trial.

6. Limitations during the trial

  • Triplem AI (the assistant inside your workspace) is not switched on automatically. Triplem VIP enables it per account on request, and it uses your own Google Gemini API key. Google may charge you for the use of your key under your own agreement with Google.
  • The Insurance module and the Classic inventory are enabled by Triplem VIP on request; they are not part of the sign-up choice.
  • An internet connection is required to sign in, to sync and to back up. Recently opened records and pending changes are kept on the device during short outages and sync when you are back online.
  • The trial is offered for evaluation, as is. Keep your own copies of important data: you can export records and download a backup at any time.
  • Plans, prices and promotions shown during the trial apply to purchases made while they are shown.

7. How we tell you before anything changes

  • From the first day of the trial, a banner in your account shows the days that remain, the end date, and a link to these terms.
  • When the trial ends, the banner and a notice explain that the grace period has started and show the exact date and time it ends, and what will change after it.
  • When the grace period ends, the banner and a notice explain that the account has limited access, what still works and what is paused, and how to restore full access.
  • Account Settings › Plan & Subscription always shows your plan, its end date, the end of the grace period and your current access.

8. When the trial ends: the grace period

The grace period starts when the trial ends and lasts 3 days (72 hours). During the grace period everything works exactly as during the trial. Choosing a plan during the grace period avoids any interruption.

9. After the grace period: limited access

If no plan has been approved when the grace period ends, the account moves to limited access. It stays active and is not switched off, and it keeps every record. Limited access lasts until a plan is approved or until you ask us to close the account.

Still available during limited access:

  • signing in on all your devices and apps, as usual;
  • viewing, searching, filtering and sorting every record in every section;
  • opening and downloading reports, statements, PDFs, CSV and Excel exports, receipts, invoices, documents and attachments;
  • downloading account backups;
  • Account Settings, password and security settings, notifications, Live Support and messages;
  • choosing and paying for a plan in Plan & Subscription.

Paused until a plan is approved:

  • adding new entries in any section, including expenses, income, wallets, transfers, inventory items, stock, sales, purchases, payments, loans, installments, assets, depreciation, notes and reminders, accounting entries and documents, and insurance records;
  • editing, archiving or deleting existing entries;
  • importing data, restoring a backup into the account, uploading new attachments or photos, saving entries offline for later and creating AI drafts.

Limited access applies to the whole business account on every device and app, including all team members of a company account. It is enforced by our servers, so a change that is paused cannot be saved by any other route.

Reminders you created before keep being delivered. Triplem VIP administrators can still make support corrections on request.

11. Your data

  • Everything you enter remains yours. Triplem VIP does not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can export your records and download a backup at any time: during the trial, the grace period and limited access.
  • Triplem VIP does not delete records because a trial or plan has ended.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and records at any time: email info@triplem.vip from your registered email address. We delete them within 30 days of a verified request, except information we must keep by law.
  • Details: Privacy Policy, https://triplem.vip/seo/privacy-policy.html (Microsoft Store edition: https://triplem.vip/windows-store/privacy.html).

12. Renewals and refunds

  • Paid plans do not renew automatically. Before a plan ends you are reminded in the app, and you renew it yourself.
  • When a paid plan ends, the same grace period and limited access apply as after a trial.
  • Payment questions and refund requests: billing@triplem.vip. They are handled under the Terms and Conditions and the consumer law that applies to you.

13. Windows app from the Microsoft Store

  • The Windows app is free to download from the Microsoft Store. It opens your Triplem VIP account, so a Triplem VIP account and an internet connection are required.
  • The free trial and paid plans belong to your Triplem VIP account, not to the Microsoft Store. Payments for a plan go directly to Triplem VIP; Microsoft is not the seller of Triplem VIP plans and does not process them.
  • Updates for the Microsoft Store edition come only from the Microsoft Store.
  • The Microsoft Store edition does not include cryptocurrency features. Records created elsewhere stay in your account and are not changed.

14. Ending the trial early, misuse and changes

  • You do not need to cancel: if you do not choose a plan, the trial ends as described above and nothing is charged.
  • To close your account at any time, contact info@triplem.vip or Live Support.
  • We may suspend an account that breaks the Terms and Conditions, for example fraud, abuse of the service or repeated trials for the same person or business.
  • We may change or end the free trial offer for future sign-ups. A change never shortens a trial that has already started, and we will tell signed-in users about material changes to these terms.
