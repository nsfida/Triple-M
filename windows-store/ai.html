AI features and how to report AI content

Triplem VIP includes live generative AI. This page explains where it is used and how to report a problem.

Where AI is used

  • Aziz and the Live Support assistant answer questions about Triplem VIP on the website and in the app. You can ask for a human agent at any time.
  • Triplem AI answers questions about your own workspace (for example spending this month or open loans) and can prepare drafts. It is enabled per account on request and uses your own Google Gemini API key.
  • Answers are generated live by Google Gemini models in response to what you type. They are labelled as AI.

Limits and safeguards

  • AI answers can be wrong or incomplete. Check important figures in your records before you rely on them.
  • Triplem AI only reads data you are allowed to see, cannot change finalised records and only creates drafts that you review and finalise yourself.
  • The assistants answer only about Triplem VIP and your workspace and decline other topics.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs, Authenticator codes or recovery keys with an assistant; it never needs them.

Report AI content

You can report any AI answer that is offensive, harmful, sexual, misleading, inaccurate or a privacy concern:

  • Every AI answer has a Report button under it.
  • Every AI screen has a permanent Report an issue link and a flag button in its header, also before any answer is shown.
  • In the Windows app: Windows app menu › Report AI content.
  • Or email info@triplem.vip.

Reports go to the Triplem VIP team with the reported answer and the question before it. The team reviews every report, records the outcome and acts on it, for example by correcting the assistant's knowledge or instructions, blocking the content or, where needed, restricting AI access for an account.

Data sent to the AI provider

Your question, the recent conversation and, for Triplem AI, the workspace data needed to answer are sent to Google to generate the reply. See the Privacy Policy.
