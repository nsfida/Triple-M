Triplem VIP for Windows, Microsoft Store edition

A private workspace for expenses, inventory, assets, loans, installments, accounting and reports, as a Windows app. Requires a Triplem VIP account (free 14-day trial) and an internet connection.

Before you install

  • A Triplem VIP account is required. You can create one in the app with a free 14-day trial (no card). The app opens your account at triplem.vip.
  • An internet connection is required to sign in, sync and back up. Recently opened records stay available during short outages and sync later.
  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11 (x64 or Arm64).
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (included with Windows 11 and current versions of Windows 10).
  • Generative AI: the app includes AI assistants (Google Gemini). See AI features.

What you can do

Expenses & Wallets

Record spending and income, keep wallets and bank accounts in step, move money between them and keep receipts with each entry.

Inventory 2.0

Products and variants, stock by location, purchases, sales, point of sale, barcodes and labels, stock reports.

Assets

An asset register with depreciation schedules.

Loans and Installments

Money lent and borrowed, repayment plans, due dates and balances.

Accounting (optional)

Double-entry books, chart of accounts, journals and statements.

Notes & Reminders

Notes, checklists and reminders linked to your work.

Reports and PDF export

Dashboards, period reports, a financial audit view and PDF/CSV exports.

Multi-currency

Workspaces in AED, SAR, USD, PKR, EUR, INR and more, with regional billing.

Windows integration

Windows notifications, taskbar jump list, tray icon, Windows Hello app lock, keyboard shortcuts, backups to a folder you choose and an offline copy of recent records.

Free trial, grace period and limited access

New accounts start with a free trial. In short:

  1. Days 1 to 14 · Free trialFull access to the modules you choose (by default Expenses & Wallets, Inventory 2.0, Assets, Loans, Installments and Notes; Accounting is optional). No card and no automatic charge; the trial never turns into a paid plan by itself.
  2. Next 3 days · Grace periodEverything keeps working. The app shows the exact date and time the grace period ends.
  3. Afterwards · Limited accessThe account stays active and nothing is deleted. You can sign in, view, search and download every record, report, PDF, CSV export, receipt, invoice, document and backup. New entries, changes and deletions are paused in every section until a Pro plan is approved.
  4. Any time · Full access againChoose Pro Monthly or Pro Yearly in Plan & Subscription; full access returns as soon as the payment is approved.
  • Limits during the trial: Triplem AI is enabled on request and uses your own Google Gemini API key; the Insurance module and the Classic inventory are enabled on request; an internet connection is required.
  • A banner in the app counts down the remaining days, explains the grace period and limited access, and links to the full Free Trial Terms.

Updates

The Microsoft Store edition is updated only by the Microsoft Store. It has no updater of its own and never downloads or installs other software. Windows installs Store updates automatically, or you can check in the Microsoft Store under Library.

Features not included in this edition

The Microsoft Store edition does not include cryptocurrency features. They are not available, shown or described in the app. Records created elsewhere stay in your account and are not changed.

What the app does on your PC

  • Installs per user from the Microsoft Store and needs no administrator rights.
  • Opens your Triplem VIP account in a secure window (Microsoft Edge WebView2) and only loads triplem.vip and its own help pages.
  • Optional features you control in the Windows app menu: Windows notifications, keep running in the tray, start with Windows (off by default), Windows Hello app lock, backups to a folder you choose.
  • Keeps its settings and an offline copy of recent records in its own app storage in your Windows profile. Uninstalling the app removes them; your account stays online.
