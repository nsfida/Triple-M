Privacy Policy

For the Windows app from the Microsoft Store and the Triplem VIP account it opens.

Who we are

Triplem VIP is operated by Nadeem Shahzad Fida, who is responsible for the information described here. Contact: info@triplem.vip. This policy covers the Windows app from the Microsoft Store; the general policy is at triplem.vip/seo/privacy-policy.html.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, username, email address, mobile number and, for company accounts, company name, address and optional tax registration number and logo.
  • Records you create: expenses, income, wallets, inventory, customers, loans, installments, assets, accounting entries, notes, attachments and photos.
  • Security data: passwords are stored only as salted hashes; two-factor settings, passkeys, sign-in sessions and security events.
  • Support conversations with Live Support and the AI assistants, and AI content reports you send.
  • Payment requests: the plan, amount, chosen bank account and the transfer receipt you attach. We never ask for card numbers.
  • Windows app diagnostics: an error report is created only when you choose "Create error report"; personal details are removed from it before you decide whether to send it.

How we use information

  • To provide, secure and support your workspace, sync your devices and approve payments.
  • We do not sell personal or financial information and do not use it for advertising.
  • Records are isolated per account and organisation; other customers cannot see them.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, file storage and server functions that hold your account and records.
  • Google Gemini API: when you use an AI assistant, your question and the context needed to answer it are sent to Google to generate the reply. Triplem AI uses your own Gemini API key.
  • Microsoft: the Microsoft Store distributes and updates the app; Windows delivers notifications you enable. Microsoft's own privacy statement applies to the Store.
  • Website hosting and content delivery networks serve the website files, fonts and icons.

Data on your PC

The app keeps its settings, a sign-in session and an offline copy of recently opened records in its own app storage in your Windows user profile, so it is fast and works through short outages. Backups are written only to a folder you choose. Uninstalling the app removes its app storage; your account and records stay online.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while your account exists, including after a trial or plan ends; they are not deleted automatically. You can export them and make backups at any time. To delete your account and records, email info@triplem.vip from your registered email address; we delete them within 30 days of a verified request, except information we must keep by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data and to object to processing. Contact info@triplem.vip; we respond within 30 days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Access is protected by hashed passwords, optional Authenticator two-factor sign-in and passkeys, session controls, database row-level security and, in the Windows app, an optional Windows Hello app lock. Details: triplem.vip/Security/.

Children

Triplem VIP is a business tool for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We update the date on this page when the policy changes and notify signed-in users of material changes.
